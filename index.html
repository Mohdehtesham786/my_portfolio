About

// PROFILE

Nearly three years into building for the web, I care about the unglamorous half of a site that decides whether it actually works — how fast it loads, how it reads on a mid-range phone, whether a client can update it themselves next year without calling me.

Most of my day lives inside WordPress — Elementor, Divi, and Avia Builder — turning a Figma file or a rough idea into a page that's responsive, SEO-ready, and easy to hand off. I've built for roofing contractors, biology labs, interior decorators, logistics companies, and art schools. Different worlds, same discipline: clean structure first, decoration second.

Based in Bijnor, Uttar Pradesh — open to remote WordPress and frontend projects.
